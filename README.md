One image, the whole of Thursday 19 November 2026: 39 verified stories drawn and typeset as a single front page. 3 hero panels lead — the first Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral in Shenzhen, GTA VI's worldwide launch, and Anthropic's record IPO — above 12 pictured panels, a dot-matrix world map whose numbered pins match the stories, and 24 numbered briefs from Washington to Wellington.

A numbers edition in navy: the day in figures first, a UTC timeline band across the top, and maximum story density — heroes, pictured panels, a world map with numbered pins, and briefs.

Every story is drawn from the day's reporting (BBC, Reuters, AP, Al Jazeera, Le Monde, CBS, Japan Times wires); nothing is invented and no live clock times are claimed. `main.py` validates the image, prints the 39-story index, and copies `entry.png` to the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
